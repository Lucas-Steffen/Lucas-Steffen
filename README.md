## About me

Backend engineer based in Sinop, MT (Brazil). I build APIs and integrations that stay reliable under load and are easy to operate and extend.

- Currently at **VF PAR**, working on backend services for mobility operations and internal systems.
- Day to day: **TypeScript**, **NestJS**, **PostgreSQL**, **Redis/BullMQ**, **Docker** and **AWS**.
- Focus: ERP and payment integrations, asynchronous processing, access control and data protection (LGPD).
- Also write **Java**, mostly in open-source telematics code.

<br>

## What I've been working on

- **ERP integration (SAP Business One):** end-to-end settlement and posting flow, 1,000+ settlements and 4,000+ postings per day with no manual entry. BullMQ/Redis workers with configurable rate limit and concurrency protect the ERP during peaks.
- **Payments:** Pix and installment card charges through Asaas, with idempotent webhooks and append-only records.
- **Data protection:** PII encryption with AWS KMS envelope encryption, aligned with LGPD.
- **Open source, Java:** contributions to a [Traccar](https://github.com/traccar/traccar) fork: a new tracker protocol decoder, PostgreSQL support and multi-tenancy
  ([server](https://github.com/VFPAR/traccar-server/tree/integration/micodus-mv730g) · [web](https://github.com/VFPAR/traccar-web/tree/feat/micodus-integration)).
